# Reflective journals

Open **QA Control** on Aurora. On the assessment list, the journal button opens the available ongoing journal. If several are available, choose the pencil for the one you want.

On the journal page, confirm the detected subject and module. If detection is unavailable, choose them from the dropdowns. Add your own class experience and feelings, then click **Prepare journal draft** or press **Alt + Shift + J**.

Review and edit the five draft sections in Browser Assistant. The counters show words per section. The copy control checks for 80 words per section and 400 total. Follow the portal's instructions when entering your reflection, accepting its declaration and submitting it.

The popup shows recorded portal countdowns, including on other websites. Each journal keeps its own timer. A timer is recorded once the portal displays an unambiguous countdown. The portal controls the official time and confirms submission. Keep the journal page open if you want its latest state reflected in the popup.

The five supported subject syllabi are loaded from `server/journal_syllabus.json`. Changes to this file are read on the next request.
